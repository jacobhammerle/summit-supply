Test the settings flow.

1. Open the app. Tap "Settings".
2. In the Units segmented control, select "Metric".
3. Turn the "Notifications" toggle OFF.
4. Turn the "Trip reminders" toggle ON.
5. Tap "Save changes".
6. Verify the screen shows "Settings saved" and the summary
   "Units: metric · Notifications: off · Reminders: on".

Pass condition: both the "Settings saved" text and the exact summary
line are visible.
